# The one prompt

Everything the editor needs, in a single call. Fill the bracketed parts from the episode and
send it once. Splitting this into passes is what makes a cut expensive: the editor re-reads the
whole transcript on every call, so five small asks cost five full reads.

Measured 2026-08-30 on a 35-minute take: five passes cost 271 credits. The same work stated
once costs roughly 60.

Two brackets have to be filled from the transcript, which already exists and is free to read.
Do that before you send this. See step 2 of the skill.

---

Cut this recording. It is one continuous [N]-minute take of a scripted episode, recorded alone,
and it is full of retakes. Leave the last good attempt of every line and remove everything else.
Then correct the transcript's mishearings, then turn on captions. Do all three now; I am not
coming back for a second pass.

THE RULES, in priority order.

1. KEEP THE BEST ATTEMPT, which is almost always the last. Wherever the same sentence appears
   two or more times in a row, delete every earlier attempt. A founder often goes three or four times,
   so follow the chain to the final one rather than stopping at the first pair. The exception:
   when an earlier attempt is grammatically correct and the later one is not, keep the earlier.

1a. THE OPENING GETS ITS OWN PASS. The opening line gets said several times and the first fifteen
   seconds carry the video. Find every attempt, work through to the LAST one, and then discard
   any candidate that holds a pause over one second anywhere inside it, even when it is the
   latest and the only complete one. A fluent fragment beats a complete take that stalls. If
   every attempt stalls, cut the stall out of the middle of the best one.

2. "ONE MORE" IS A KILL SIGNAL. When the speaker says "One more." on its own, they are
   telling themselves the take they just did was bad. Delete that phrase and the attempt
   before it. Treat any similar aside to themselves the same way.

3. DELETE ASIDES TO THE EDITOR. The speaker sometimes breaks character to give editing directions by
   name. Those come out whole.

4. A STALLING TAKE LOSES TO A FLUENT ONE, even when the stalling one is the only complete one.
   If an attempt freezes for seconds mid-sentence, prefer another. If the stalling one is all
   there is, cut the freeze out of its middle and keep the words.

5. REMOVE SILENCE OVER 0.3 SECONDS. Tighter than your default, and deliberate. The room has a
   high noise floor so the dead parts are not literally silent. Keep the breath at sentence
   boundaries and close everything else.

   A lone "You" or "Thank you" sitting over keyboard or mouse noise, with no sentence around it,
   is a transcription hallucination and not something that was said. Delete the phantom word and close
   the whole stretch around it as one dead gap. Left in, those words break a two-minute silence
   into short ones and it survives.

   Where the speaker is silent for minutes and the screen is STATIC, they are waiting; that comes out
   entirely. Where something is moving on screen through the silence, that is demo footage and
   it stays at full length, because it gets sped up later.

6. REMOVE FILLER AND FALSE STARTS. "uh", "um", and half-word stumbles. Keep the completed
   version, drop the aborted fragment.

7. NEVER DELETE A LINE SAID ONLY ONCE. If you cannot tell whether something is a retake or a
   new sentence, keep it. Before you finish, re-read the result and check that no sentence got
   orphaned or half-removed by one of your own cuts, especially where a line existed ONLY inside
   a take you deleted. Fix any you find. This is the one failure that cannot be repaired later.

8. DO NOT REWRITE THE SPEAKER. No paraphrasing, no reordering, no added words, and never
   correct their grammar. The video is them, and plenty of founders are not native speakers.
   You are only removing.

THEN, TEXT ONLY, no audio change and no change to the duration, correct these mishearings:
[one line per mishearing, as: what the transcript says -> what it should say]

THEN turn captions on with this styling and nothing else: [font] at [size], white text on black
at 70 percent opacity, bold, bottom centre, no outline, no shadow, no rounded corners, no
animation, no word highlighting. Do not try to control where the captions break. That is handled
outside this call and I do not want you spending the attempt.

REPORT BACK: the new duration, how many removals you made, every place you were unsure whether
something was a retake or real content, and anything above you could not do. Do not report a
change as done unless you have confirmed it in the result; a claimed fix that did not land costs
another call to find.
