# Recording for the cut

1 take, 1 file. Your screen, with your face in a small bubble in the corner.

## Before you press record

- Notifications off.
- Every window your script names is open. The `[SCREEN:]` lines in the script are the list.
- The script sits where you can read it without turning your head.

## While you record

- **A slip: stop. Count 1 full second of silence. Then say the whole sentence again, from the start.**
- No sorry, no oops between tries. Anything you say between tries is 1 more thing to delete by hand.
- Never stop the recording. 1 take, 1 file.
- Read the script. A line you wander off is a line the read cannot match against the script.

## Then

Drop the file into the episode folder your script landed in, and take it through Descript:

1. Drag the raw take in.
2. Open the AI Tools panel. Remove filler words. Remove retakes, which marks your earlier tries for
   removal and keeps the last one. Shorten word gaps.
3. Export, Subtitles, SRT, saved into the same episode folder.

Then type: Cut my raw take.
